About Us

We supply high-quality steel products through an extensive and reliable network across Europe. At R-METAL, we view our relationships with clients and suppliers as our true capital.

From flat rolled steel for the automotive sector to heavy structural materials for construction and marine environments, we ensure the right grades are delivered precisely when and where they are needed.

Materials & Typical Grades

1. Construction & Infrastructure +
  • ASTM A36 / S235JR / SS400 – General structural shapes
  • ASTM A572 Gr.50 / S355JR – High-strength structural steel
  • ASTM A992 – Wide-flange beams
  • ASTM A588 / Corten – Weathering steel
  • ASTM A615 Gr.60 / B500B – Rebar
  • ASTM A53 / API 5L Gr.B – Pipe & piling
2. Automotive +
  • DP590, DP780, DP980, TRIP690, TRIP780, MS1200, MS1500 – Advanced High-Strength Steels
  • 22MnB5 / Usibor 1500P – Boron steel (hot-stamping)
  • HSLA340, HSLA420 – HSLA grades
  • SAE 4140, 8620, 5160/9260, 52100 – Engineering, spring, & bearing steels
  • 409, 439 – Exhaust stainless
3. Shipbuilding & Marine +
  • ABS A/B/D/E, AH32/DH32/EH32, AH36/DH36/EH36 – Hull plates
  • 9% Ni steel (ASTM A353/A553 Type I) – Cryogenic for LNG tanks
  • 316L (1.4404) – Marine stainless
  • S355ML, S460ML (EN 10225) – Higher strength for offshore
4. Solar & HVAC (Energy) +
  • Structural Steels – General grades for frames & racking
  • 304, 316L – Stainless steels for weather resistance, mounting systems, heat exchangers
  • Electrical Steels – Non-oriented and grain-oriented for motors/transformers
5. Food & Beverage (Hygienic) +
  • AISI 304 (1.4301) – Tanks, pipework, worktables, dairy equipment
  • AISI 316L (1.4404) – Aggressive cleaning, pharmaceutical WFI systems
  • AISI 316Ti (1.4571) – Brewery vessels, high-temp applications
  • EN 1.4006 (AISI 410) / EN 1.4116 (X50CrMoV15) – Martensitic grades for pump shafts and premium knives
